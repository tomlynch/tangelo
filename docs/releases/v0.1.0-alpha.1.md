The first public alpha of **Tangelo**, a modern Mac media player powered by VLC's playback engine (libVLC).

> **This is an alpha.** Expect rough edges and bugs, and keep VLC around for anything critical. Please
> [open an issue](https://github.com/tomlynch/tangelo/issues) when something doesn't work, including the file type
> and what you expected to happen.

### Install

1. Download **Tangelo-v0.1.0-alpha.1.dmg** below.
2. Open it and drag Tangelo to Applications.
3. Launch it. The app is signed and notarized by Apple, so it opens without security warnings.

Requires **macOS 15 or later**, on Apple silicon or Intel.

### What's in this alpha

- Plays the formats and streams VLC 3 plays: MKV, MP4, AVI, HEVC, AV1, FLAC, subtitles, network shares and more
- Edge-to-edge video with auto-hiding glass controls, seek previews and a floating mini player
- Continue Watching, resume where you left off, and automatic queueing of the next episode
- Chromecast for any format; AirPlay for MP4, MOV and M4V
- Browsing of shared folders (SMB) and media servers (DLNA/UPnP) on your network
- Now Playing, media keys, AirPods controls, and trackpad gestures

### Known limitations

- No DVD or Blu-ray disc support (ripped files play fine)
- AirPlay works only with MP4, MOV and M4V; use Chromecast for other formats
- No automatic updates yet; check this page for new versions
- Casting, AirPlay and media keys have had limited testing on real hardware

### About the engine

Tangelo bundles VideoLAN's VLCKit 3.7.3 (libVLC 3.0.x), unmodified, under the LGPL 2.1 or later. Its source is
attached to this release (`VLCKit-source-*.tar.gz` and `vlc-source-*.tar.gz`). Tangelo is not affiliated with or
endorsed by VideoLAN.

Verify downloads against `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
